# Dated correction to the DDT proposal for TIC 80387959 (2026-10-21)

The Director's Discretionary Time proposal for 2-minute cadence on TIC 80387959 (Sectors 111-114) was submitted to the TESS DDT office on
2026-10-19. The proposal itself is not in this repository and is kept unchanged. This note corrects how two of its statements should be read.

1. **"The candidate was identified on 2026 October 18 in a multi-year search of QLP light curves."** This is when our own search found
   the signal. It is an independent recovery: the same signal (P = 4.1922173881 d) had already been reported in the first-stage RAVEN
   candidate catalog of Lafarga et al. (2026, MNRAS, doi:10.1093/mnras/stag512; online material Zenodo 19661443). It is absent from
   their vetted and validated subsets. We do not claim first detection. See prior_search/ERRATUM_PRIOR_DETECTIONS_2026-10-21.md.
2. **"The star ... has no TOI, CTOI or SPOC threshold-crossing event."** This remains true for TOIs, CTOIs and SPOC 2-minute
   threshold-crossing events. Separately, the TESS-SPOC full-frame-image pipeline produced an unpromoted single-sector alert at the same
   period in Sector 63, which the proposal did not mention.

Neither earlier detection establishes a planet, and neither changes the request: 2-minute data in Sectors 111-114 would test the frozen
predictions (preregistration/TIC80387959/). No separate message has been sent to the DDT office; this correction will be mentioned if the
office replies.
