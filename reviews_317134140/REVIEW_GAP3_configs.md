## Fidelity and design-sector history

The preparation-order claim is supported by the source. `scripts/blind_seasons_317134140.py:11–16` and `final_ephemeris_317134140.py:19–25` remove TOI-2514.01 before flattening, with the full 1.5×4.047-hour window. The current scorer uses that order/window (`prereg_spoc_317134140.py:43–52`). It deliberately omits the candidate transit mask, unlike those fitting scripts, and uses the supplied PDCSAP errors rather than their empirical constant errors. Its clipping MAD is also computed on all retained finite flattened points rather than the candidate-excluded subset. Those differences are visible in source; “mirrors the preparation” should be understood with these qualifications, not as byte-identical preprocessing.

The blind search itself matches the original **per-sector** search: 6-minute bins, the same duration grid, minimum period 0.5 d, maximum `max(1,baseline/2)`, oversampling 3, and the same `bls_stats` function (`search_pipeline.py:90–96`; scorer lines 80–89). Peaks are selected by the existing detrended/robust SDE ranking, with >1% period separation (`search_pipeline.py:41–67`). I verified `tsearch.py`, `search_pipeline.py` and `blind_seasons_317134140.py` are byte-identical to the published copies.
