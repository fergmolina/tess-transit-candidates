## 1. Verdict

**Publish after listed fixes. Do not publish in the current form.**

Conditions:
1. Every must-fix below must be published **before any Sector 106 photometry is examined**. Several items (the governing-hierarchy statement, the 70441745 timing/depth rule, the duration and INCONSISTENT disclosures) cannot be fixed afterwards without looking outcome-driven.
2. Any change to `prereg_conformance.py` or to the configs changes the SHA-256s pinned at `CONFORMANCE_PROCEDURE_2026-10-20.md:17-22`. After a code change, the authors must re-run the synthetic suite and the calibration, update the hashes, and have the changed document re-reviewed.
3. The missing review dimensions must be merged into this report before final sign-off.

Why not "do not publish": no finding shows an outcome-driven choice. All reference values and configs were locked before any S106 data were examined. Every upheld item is one of three things: a disclosure or hierarchy gap, a calibration that must be re-run with the locked settings, or a single rule decision that must be declared. Why not "publish as declared": the document says it "implements the written protocol outcome rules" (`prereg_conformance.py:4`). In at least five places it silently departs from `PREREG_PROTOCOL.tex`, and it does not say which document governs product and reference values.

## 4. Refuted findings and refuted sub-claims

No finding came back upheld=false in the visible input. The following claims were refuted by at least one skeptic and must not appear in the published fix list:
- U2 as a blocker against a "governing" frozen EXCLUDED: refuted by one skeptic. The frozen EXCLUDED comes from baseline contamination, the registration defines RECOVERED by box position, and the precedence is declared. The finding stands as an undeclared departure (major).
- "Zero overlap for 45 < |o| <= 130 min" (U2): wrong. Zero overlap starts at |o| >= T14 = 90 min.
- "The code settles the RECOVERED/INCONSISTENT overlap without saying so" (U2): wrong. Docstring l.33-39 and `CONFORMANCE_PROCEDURE:32-35` state it.
- "Incoherent displacements can never return INCONSISTENT" (U5): wrong. +40/+60 min and a 40-90 min drift returned INCONSISTENT. Only spreads of about T14/2 or more fail, and those go to EXCLUDED.
- A wrong period as a realistic cause of incoherence (U5): to break coherence within one sector the period would need to be about 1700 (120444365), 2400 (183374279) or 240 (70441745) sigma_P off.
- "Requiring fixed-phase SNR costs no power in-sample" (max-SNR finding): wrong. It gives 84/100 vs 90/100 calibration injections and 2/3 vs 3/3 real injections.
- "Both in-sample real transits sit at the band edge" (U3): wrong for S105 (1.15x). True for S96 only.
- "The procedure doc labels 1090 / 2.84 'frozen registration line'" (U1): it says "frozen registration line and addendum" (l.44). The config's `reference_source` does say "(frozen)".
