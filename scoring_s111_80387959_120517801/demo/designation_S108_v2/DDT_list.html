The table below summarizes the Director's Discretionary Target proposal for the entirety of the TESS mission. Included are DDT number, name of the DDT principal investigator, the subject of the DDT, the cadence requested (20s or 2m), and the number of targets. A link to the DDT candidate target list is provided for each DDT.

Note that all targets observed at 20s cadence are also observed at 2m cadence.

If there are questions or comments, please contact Joel Villasenor (jsvilla@space.mit.edu) or Roland Vanderspek (roland@space.mit.edu).
